Leo Inbox · Privacy notice

Privacy Notice

What Leo Inbox accesses

Leo Inbox uses the Gmail API with the gmail.readonly scope. It reads message headers, content, labels and threads in the authorised mailbox. It cannot send, delete, archive or mark messages as read, modify labels, or write to Google Calendar.

The owner signs in with Google OAuth. The application does not ask for or store account passwords.

Where data is stored and processed

Refresh tokens and local processing records are kept in access-restricted files on the owner's computer. These records may contain message text, model prompts and results, priority decisions and follow-up state.

Some email content and context is sent to OpenAI (Sol) for analysis, or to Anthropic (Sonnet) as a fallback, using the owner's existing provider accounts. Selected alert summaries may be sent to the owner through Telegram. These services process submitted data under their applicable terms and the owner's account settings.

Provider settings must prohibit training on submitted mailbox content before live review is enabled. This is an operational prerequisite, not a claim that settings have already been verified.

How data is used

All processing is solely for the owner's inbox triage. Leo Inbox does not sell mailbox data, use it for advertising, or train generalised AI models.

Google API Services Limited Use disclosure

Leo Inbox's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping and deleting

The owner may stop scheduled checks at any time, revoke Google third-party access and delete local files. Revoking Google access stops future reads but does not delete local copies. Local records remain until the owner deletes or clears them. Retention by third-party providers follows their terms and the owner's account settings.

This website

This public site contains only application information. No mailbox data or credentials are hosted here. No analytics or tracking is added by the application; the hosting provider may collect standard request metadata.

Contact

For general questions, open an issue at GitHub repository issues. Never post email content or credentials there.
