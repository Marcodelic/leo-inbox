Leo Inbox

Leo Inbox is a personal, read-only email triage tool. It connects to the owner’s authorised Gmail accounts through Google OAuth and reads message headers, content, labels and threads to identify what needs the owner's attention: pending replies, decisions, meetings, deadlines, tax and legal items, security alerts, and client or work matters.

It does not send, delete, archive or modify anything in the mailbox. Selected message context is sent to OpenAI or Anthropic for analysis, and alert summaries may be delivered to the owner through Telegram.

Leo Inbox is not a public signup service. It is built and operated for one account owner's use.

The tool is being configured. Authorisation happens through Google, not through this website.
